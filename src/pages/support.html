Join the crew

There's a place for you

Metal fabricator, LED wizard, rigger, or someone who just shows up and makes things happen: we need people in New York and on the playa.

Build in NYC

Metal fabrication, LED soldering, lighting programming and our test builds. You don't need to go to Burning Man to be part of this.

Build on playa

Arrive for build week and help raise a sixty-foot cathedral from the dust: scaffolding, rigging, LEDs, MOOP and camp operations.

Camp with us

Core playa builders can camp with us at Deli Art Support, next to NYC Deli: potable water, showers, kitchen, shade and power in every tent.
