Folly Mix · lighting software

Play the structure like an instrument

Folly Mix is the lighting software we custom-coded for the Gothic Folly. It addresses every arch, spire, tower and rose-window cell individually, so a look can range from a wash over the whole structure to a single arch pulsing in one colour on the off-beat. It runs on a phone, iPad or tablet.

In use

Building a look

Fifteen seconds in Folly Mix: four colours are set on the palette wheel, then Stained glass, Arch chase, Rose mandala and a Spire flash accent are layered on top. The 3D preview on the right shows the result on the structure.

Mapping

How the structure is mapped

Rather than treating the Folly as a grid of pixels, Folly Mix is written around its geometry. Effects are addressed to its parts: the nave arches, the towers, the spires and spirelets, the rose window and each cell of its tracery.

Targeting

Send any effect to the whole cathedral, the arches only, the towers without the arches, the spires, the tower quads or the rose window alone. The Parts row gives each section its own effect, so the spires can sweep while the arches glow.

Pad settings

Every pad is editable: its effect, where it plays, its direction, its colours, its speed and its size. Effects lock to the beat or the tempo, and launch now, on the next beat or on the next bar.

Manual control

Paint light by hand across the structure or cell by cell on the rose window. Filters switch off specific lights whatever is playing, and gamma calibration makes the real window match what you see on screen.

Any look can be saved as a scene, and scenes can be chained into a timed show that plays back the same way each night.

Features

How Folly Mix is organised

Folly Mix is split into five pages. Their output layers together, and everything follows one adjustable global tempo.

01 · Mix

Layered looks

Looks are built on the Mix page. Each row is a layer aimed at part of the structure, with its own fader, mute and blend mode, so a base wash, motion on the arches and detail on the rose window can be stacked and crossfaded.

  • Base: nave glow, a stained-glass effect, plasma, aurora, fire and palette drift across the whole cathedral
  • Motion: sweeps, falling bars, arch chases, comets, ripples and pinwheels
  • Rose: spins, rings, twinkle and mandalas on the window alone
  • Accents, Textures and Parts: beat sparkles, spire flashes, strobes, shimmer and per-section control

01 · Mix, continued

Colour and beat overlays

Colour comes from a single hue on the wheel or a four-colour palette, chosen from presets or generated harmonies. Beat overlays run across the whole structure, and master faders shape the result.

  • Searchlights
  • Bipolar
  • Levels
  • Waves
  • Bubbles
  • Wander beams
  • Turret
  • Faders for dimmer, speed, hue, colour, fade, power cap and gamma
  • Launch pads now, on the next beat, or on the next bar

02 · Drum

Drum pads

The Drum page maps each tower, spire, arch group and the rose window to a pad. Pads can be hit, toggled or held over the mix, with a crossfader between the two.

  • Build starts a riser that is dragged up into a drop
  • Blackout, Pulse and Freak out are momentary effects
  • A loop recorder captures 1 to 8 bars of drumming and plays it back in time

03 · Draw

Drawing

A brush dragged across an elevation of the structure paints light directly onto it. A second canvas paints the rose window cell by cell, in the live palette.

04 · Animations

Animations and shows

Hand-built animations play on the rose window, each with a matching preset for the rest of the structure. A text mode scrolls any message through the window. Timed shows run the whole Folly unattended.

  • Shows include The Spark, Meteor Shower, Tornado, NYC Skyline, Vespers, Candle Vigil, Tide, Ascension and the Vampire Ball set

05 · Sequences

Scenes and sequences

Looks are saved as scenes, then chained with shows and animations into a sequence. Each step plays for a set number of bars and fades into the next, and the whole sequence follows the global tempo.

  • Save, duplicate and rename sequences
  • Back up and restore your library to a file
